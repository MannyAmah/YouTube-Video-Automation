YouTube Medical Educational Stories

This is an 100% Automated YouTube Channel for Medical and Health related educational stories. It will focus on medications and diseases. Using stories and detailed illustrations to teach about medications and diseases in a more and indepth way, encompassing everything associated with them. The illustrations must be visualizable to make it feel highly interactable and relatable to real human physiology and biology.
